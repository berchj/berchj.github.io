What tinyswitch does

🖼️

Images

JPG (mozjpeg), PNG (smart palette, like TinyPNG), WebP, AVIF and TIFF.

📱

iPhone photos

HEIC photos are converted to JPG while compressing.

📄

PDF

Shrinks the photos inside scanned and photo-heavy PDFs.

📊

Word, PowerPoint, Excel

Shrinks the images inside; text and layout stay exactly the same.

🖱️

Right-click menu

Compress with tinyswitch from your file manager (Windows and Linux).

🔒

Offline

Unlike online compressors, your files never leave your computer.

How to use it

  1. Download and install tinyswitch.
  2. Drag photos, PDFs, Word or PowerPoint files (or whole folders) onto the window.
  3. A smaller copy is saved next to each one as name-min.ext.
  4. Optional: lower the quality, set a max width or convert photos to WebP.

First launch: the installers aren’t signed with a paid certificate yet. On Windows click More info → Run anyway; on macOS open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Questions

How do I reduce the size of a PDF without uploading it?

Drop it on tinyswitch. It recompresses the photos inside — what makes scanned PDFs heavy — and saves name-min.pdf next to it. Nothing leaves your computer.

How do I make a PowerPoint or Word file smaller to send it by email?

Drop it on tinyswitch. Files full of photos often shrink by half or more; text, slides and layout are unchanged.

How do I convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG?

Drop them on tinyswitch: they come out as compressed JPG files that open everywhere.

Is it like TinyPNG?

Similar results for PNG and JPG, but it runs on your computer: no upload limits, no file size limits, and private files stay private.

Does it change my original files?

Never. It always writes a new file next to the original.

Is tinyswitch free?

Yes, it’s free and open source under the MIT license.
