Questions

Is there PowerToys for Mac?

No, PowerToys is Windows-only. toolswitch offers similar everyday tools on macOS: clipboard history, OCR, file compressor, background remover and more.

Is there PowerToys for Linux?

No. On Linux you can use toolswitch, which runs on Ubuntu and other distributions with the same shortcuts.

Can I use toolswitch together with PowerToys?

Yes. On Windows they can run side by side; you can turn off the toolswitch tools you already have in PowerToys.

The 9 tools

📋

Clipboard history

Everything you copy, text and images, one shortcut away. Search it and pin favorites.

🔍

Text from screen

Select part of the screen and copy the text in it (OCR). Accents, ñ and tables included.

🖼️

Wallpapers

Pick your wallpapers and switch them with a shortcut or every few minutes.

⏰

Reminders

Write “in 20 min call Juan” and get a notification then. No calendar, no account.

☕

Breaks

Rest your eyes every 20 minutes (20-20-20), or work with a Pomodoro timer.

📦

Compress files

Smaller JPG, PNG, PDF, Word, PowerPoint and Excel files. Your originals stay untouched.

✂️

Remove background

Transparent PNG of any photo, with an AI model that runs on your computer.

✒️

Sign PDF

Draw your signature once, drag it to its place on the page and sign PDFs without printing.

🎤

Audio to text

Voice notes, WhatsApp audios, recordings and videos into text, offline, in ~100 languages.
