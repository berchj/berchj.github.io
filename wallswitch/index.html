What wallswitch does

🖼️

Wallpaper carousel

Click the images you like and they rotate every 1 minute to 1 day.

🌅

Photo of the day

A new 4K photo from Bing every day, set as your wallpaper automatically.

⌨️

Keyboard shortcuts

Next, previous and random wallpaper from anywhere. Record your own keys.

🔔

Tray icon

Lives quietly in your system tray / top bar with a quick menu.

📁

Folders or single images

Uses your Pictures/Wallpapers folder and the system wallpapers by default.

💻

Command line

wallswitch next, wallswitch daily, wallswitch set photo.jpg… great for scripts.

How to use it

  1. Download and install wallswitch.
  2. Click the images you want in your carousel (blue outline).
  3. Turn on Auto change, or Photo of the day for a new wallpaper every morning.
  4. Use Super+. / Super+, (Ctrl+Alt+. on Windows/Mac) for next and previous.

First launch: the installers aren’t signed with a paid certificate yet. On Windows click More info → Run anyway; on macOS open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Questions

How do I change my wallpaper automatically on Windows 11, Mac or Ubuntu?

Install wallswitch, click the images you want and turn on Auto change. It rotates them every few minutes or hours — you choose the interval.

Can I get a new wallpaper every day?

Yes. Turn on Photo of the day and wallswitch downloads Bing’s photo of the day in 4K and sets it as your wallpaper each morning.

Can I change the wallpaper with a keyboard shortcut?

Yes: next, previous and random wallpaper have global shortcuts, and you can record your own keys from the Shortcuts panel.

Can I change the wallpaper from the terminal?

Yes: wallswitch next, wallswitch prev, wallswitch random, wallswitch daily or wallswitch set path/to/image.jpg.

Does wallswitch delete or move my images?

No. Your files are never touched; Remove only hides an image from the list.

Is wallswitch free?

Yes, it’s free and open source under the MIT license. No account, no ads.
