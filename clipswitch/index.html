How to use it

  1. Download and install clipswitch — it starts in your tray.
  2. Copy things as usual.
  3. Press Super+Shift+V (Ctrl+Alt+V on Windows/Mac).
  4. Pick an entry and paste it anywhere with Ctrl+V.

First launch: the installers aren’t signed with a paid certificate yet. On Windows click More info → Run anyway; on macOS open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Questions

How do I see my clipboard history?

Install clipswitch and press Super+Shift+V (Ctrl+Alt+V on Windows and Mac). You’ll see everything you copied, newest first, and can search it.

Does it save copied images and screenshots?

Yes. Images show up with a preview; pick one and it’s back on your clipboard, ready to paste.

How do I keep a text forever?

Pin it with the star or Ctrl+P. Pinned entries stay at the top and are never deleted, not even by Clear history.

Is my clipboard sent anywhere?

No. Everything is stored in a local file on your computer. There’s no account and no cloud.

Is there a clipboard manager for Linux and Mac like Windows’ Win+V?

Yes, clipswitch works the same on Linux, macOS and Windows, with text and images.

Is clipswitch free?

Yes, it’s free and open source under the MIT license.
