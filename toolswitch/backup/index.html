Automatic backup to an external drive — free and simple

A broken disk or a lost laptop shouldn’t take your photos and documents with it. Pick the folders and a drive; toolswitch copies them every day.

⬇ Download toolswitch
Windows, macOS and Linux
Free · no account · no ads

Shortcut: Super+Shift+K on Linux · Ctrl+Alt+K on Windows · Cmd+Option+K on Mac

What it does

By itself

Every hour, every 6 or 12 hours, or once a day. Only new and changed files are copied, so it’s quick.

Nothing is lost

Nothing is ever deleted in the backup, so a file you deleted by mistake is still there.

Plain copies

Your files are copied as they are: you can open them from the drive without any program.

Warns you

If the drive isn’t connected, it tells you.

How to use it

  1. Download and install toolswitch. It waits in your tray / top bar.
  2. Turn on Backup in toolswitch.
  3. Choose the folders to copy and where to copy them.
  4. Leave the drive connected; it runs on its own.

Use it instead of: FreeFileSync, Déjà Dup, Windows File History, manual copies.

First launch: the installers aren’t signed with a paid certificate yet. On Windows click More info → Run anyway; on macOS open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Questions

Does it compress or encrypt the backup?

No. It keeps plain copies so you can open them anywhere.
