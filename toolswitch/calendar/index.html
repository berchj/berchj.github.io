What it does

Your day at a glance

The events of today or any other day.

Create and edit events

Add, change or delete events and their guests — or just write “Lunch with Ana tomorrow 1pm”.

Heads-up

A notification 10 minutes before each event.

How to use it

  1. Download and install toolswitch. It waits in your tray / top bar.
  2. Turn on Calendar in toolswitch and connect your Google account.
  3. Press the shortcut to see your agenda or add an event.

Use it instead of: Google Calendar browser tab, GNOME Calendar, Calendar widgets.

First launch: the installers aren’t signed with a paid certificate yet. On Windows click More info → Run anyway; on macOS open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Questions

Is it off by default?

Yes. It only talks to Google after you turn it on and connect your account.
