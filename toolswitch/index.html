How to use it

  1. Download and install toolswitch. It starts in your tray / top bar.
  2. On the welcome screen, turn on the tools you want.
  3. Use them from anywhere with their shortcut, for example Super+Shift+V for the clipboard (Ctrl+Alt on Windows, Cmd+Option on Mac).
  4. Change any shortcut by clicking it and pressing the keys you like.

First launch: the installers aren’t signed with a paid certificate yet. On Windows click More info → Run anyway; on macOS open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Questions

What is toolswitch?

A small desktop app with 10 everyday tools — clipboard history, text from screen (OCR), wallpapers, reminders, breaks, file compressor, AI background remover, PDF signing, audio to text and a shut down timer — that waits in your tray.

Do the AI tools upload my files?

No. Background removal (BiRefNet), audio to text (OpenAI Whisper) and text from screen (PaddleOCR) run on your computer. Their models are downloaded once and then work offline. Your photos, recordings and screenshots never leave your computer.

Is it like Microsoft PowerToys?

It’s in the same spirit, but it works the same on Windows, macOS and Linux, and it focuses on everyday tasks instead of developer tools.

Can it transcribe WhatsApp voice notes and videos?

Yes. Save them to your computer and drag them into Audio to text; it reads Spanish, English and about 100 other languages.

How heavy is it?

The program is about 15 MB and uses about 90 MB of memory and almost no processor while it waits in the tray. A tool that’s off does nothing at all.

Is toolswitch free?

Yes, it’s free to download and use. No account, no ads. If it helps you, you can support it with a coffee.
