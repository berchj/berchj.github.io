Shutdown timer: turn off the PC later or when downloads finish

Leaving a download running at night? Falling asleep with a movie? Tell toolswitch when to turn the computer off.

⬇ Download toolswitch
Windows, macOS and Linux
Free · open source · MIT license

Shortcut: Super+Shift+Q on Linux · Ctrl+Alt+Q on Windows · Cmd+Option+Q on Mac

What it does

In a while

In 30 minutes, an hour, or what you choose.

At a time

Shut down or sleep at 23:00.

When downloads finish

It waits until the download is complete.

How to use it

  1. Download and install toolswitch. It waits in your tray / top bar.
  2. Press the shortcut or open Shut down timer.
  3. Choose shut down, restart or sleep, and when.
  4. You can cancel any time.

Use it instead of: shutdown -s -t, Wise Auto Shutdown, sleep timer apps.

First launch: the installers aren’t signed with a paid certificate yet. On Windows click More info → Run anyway; on macOS open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Questions

How do I shut down the PC after a download?

Choose “when the downloads finish” in the Shut down timer. It works with any browser or download app.
