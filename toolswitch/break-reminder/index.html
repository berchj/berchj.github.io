Break reminder and Pomodoro timer (20-20-20) — free

Hours in front of the screen tire your eyes and your back. toolswitch reminds you to look away and stretch, or keeps your Pomodoro sessions.

⬇ Download toolswitch
Windows, macOS and Linux
Free · no account · no ads

Shortcut: Super+Shift+P on Linux · Ctrl+Alt+P on Windows · Cmd+Option+P on Mac

What it does

20-20-20

Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet (6 m) away for 20 seconds.

Pomodoro

25 minutes of work, 5 of rest — or your own times.

Sounds

A soft sound when the break starts and another when it’s time to go back.

How to use it

  1. Download and install toolswitch. It waits in your tray / top bar.
  2. Turn on Breaks in toolswitch.
  3. Choose 20-20-20 or Pomodoro.
  4. Keep working; it tells you when to rest.

Use it instead of: Workrave, Stretchly, EyeLeo, Pomodoro timer websites.

First launch: the installers aren’t signed with a paid certificate yet. On Windows click More info → Run anyway; on macOS open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Questions

What is the 20-20-20 rule?

Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet (6 meters) away for 20 seconds. It’s what eye doctors recommend against eye strain from screens.
