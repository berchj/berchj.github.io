Quick desktop reminders: “in 20 min call Juan” — free

For the small things that don’t deserve a calendar event. Press a shortcut, write what and when in plain words, and forget about it until it pops up.

⬇ Download toolswitch
Windows, macOS and Linux
Free · no account · no ads

Shortcut: Super+Shift+R on Linux · Ctrl+Alt+R on Windows · Cmd+Option+R on Mac

What it does

Plain words

“in 20 min call Juan”, “at 17:30 send the report”, “tomorrow 9 pay rent”.

A notification with a sound

It shows up on your desktop at the right time.

Survives restarts

Reminders are saved; if the computer was off at that time, it shows up as soon as it’s back.

English or Spanish

“in 2 hours…”, “tomorrow 9:00 dentist”, “mañana a las 8 gym”, “en 20 min…”.

How to use it

  1. Download and install toolswitch. It waits in your tray / top bar.
  2. Press the shortcut.
  3. Write the reminder, for example in 20 min call Juan, and press Enter.
  4. You get a notification at that time.

Use it instead of: sticky notes, phone alarms, Google Keep reminders, Microsoft To Do for quick reminders.

First launch: the installers aren’t signed with a paid certificate yet. On Windows click More info → Run anyway; on macOS open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Reminders stay on your computer.

What if the computer was off at that time?

The reminder shows up as soon as toolswitch starts again.
