Small apps that do one thing well

Free and open source, for Windows, macOS and Linux. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

wallswitch

Wallpaper changer. Rotate your desktop backgrounds automatically, get a new 4K photo every day and switch wallpapers with keyboard shortcuts. Windows, macOS and Linux.

clipswitch

Clipboard manager. Keeps everything you copy (text and images), pin favorites and paste them again with one keyboard shortcut. Windows, macOS and Linux. Private and offline.

tinyswitch

Image & document compressor. Make JPG, PNG, HEIC photos, PDF, Word, PowerPoint and Excel files smaller by dragging them in. Works offline — nothing is uploaded.
