See the 14 tools →

📋

Clipboard history

Everything you copy, text and images, one shortcut away. Search it and pin favorites.

🔍

Text from screen

Select part of the screen and copy the text in it (OCR, letters without accents for now).

🖼️

Wallpapers

Pick your wallpapers and switch them with a shortcut or every few minutes.

⏰

Reminders

Write “in 20 min call Juan” and get a notification then. No calendar, no account.

☕

Breaks

Rest your eyes every 20 minutes (20-20-20), or work with a Pomodoro timer.

📦

Compress files

Smaller JPG, PNG, PDF, Word, PowerPoint and Excel files. Your originals stay untouched.

✂️

Remove background

Transparent PNG of any photo, with an AI model that runs on your computer.

✒️

Sign PDF

Draw your signature once and put it on PDFs without printing them.

📂

Tidy downloads

Moves what you download to its folder by type, by itself. Every move can be undone.

💾

Backup

Copies the folders you pick to another disk every day. Only what changed.

♻️

Clean up

Shows what takes space — trash, caches, old downloads, big files — and frees it.

📈

Watch the PC

Warns when a disk is almost full or a program gets stuck eating the processor or memory.

🔌

Shut down timer

Shut down, restart or sleep later, at a time, or when the downloads finish.

⟳

Updates

Updates the system and your apps with one click, and tells you when there’s something new.
